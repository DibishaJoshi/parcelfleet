BCA Final Year Project: Future Scope & Roadmap
BCA Final Year Defense Integration Note
This full-stack implementation serves as a functional, commercial-ready core prototype. The items below outline the future development scopes ideal for project expansions, viva defenses, and software engineering planning documentation.
Native Mobile Applications
Development of Android and iOS mobile shells for drivers (real-time telemetry background services) and customers (push notifications and mobile map sweeps).
SMS & Email Notification APIs
Integration with platforms like Twilio, Sparrow SMS, or SendGrid to broadcast SMS/Email alerts on status transitions (e.g. "OTP verification codes for delivery").
Google Maps API Suite
Upgrading OpenStreetMap Leaflet layers to Google Maps Directions API to obtain visual road traffic arrays, commercial ETA estimations, and geocoding services.
Real GPS Tracker Hardware
Configuring fleet telemetry matching with OBD-II physical GPS hardware trackers inside vehicles (e.g. Raspberry Pi or Arduino nodes transmitting MQTT messages).
AI Route & Dispatch Optimization
Applying Reinforcement Learning (RL) or advanced Genetic Algorithms to predict dispatch time bottlenecks based on vehicle type and historical valley road traffic records.
Multi-Warehouse Inventory
Building advanced stock sorting systems, enabling complex multi-warehouse sorting, parcel redistribution chains, and parcel weight checks on arrival.
